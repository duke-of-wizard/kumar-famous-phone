Kumar Famous Phone

A phone that unfolds into a tablet, with a pen in the hinge and snap-on game controllers.

  • Phone + tabletFold it to use as a phone. Unfold both sides for one big tablet screen.
  • Pen in the hingePull it out to write and draw. It charges while stored.
  • Game modeSnap on two controllers and it's a game console.
  • All your appsGames, messages, music, maps, camera and more.
